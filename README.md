# TFA Splits Tracker

Track your Fractured Archive raids with a sidebar showing room splits, total raid times, party members, and daily raid history.

## Raid timing

- Automatically tracks challenge starts, room completions, and raid finishes.
- Shows live room and total raid clocks during your run.
- Saves server-reported room durations, cumulative splits, and total raid time.
- Separates combat time from time spent between rooms in fully recorded raids.
- Shows your completed raid count and best total time from fully recorded runs.
- Keeps tracking after a player death while the party continues the challenge.

Live clocks are estimates between updates; completed splits use the times reported by the game. Raids with missing room times are marked as partial history and excluded from the best-total statistic.

## Raid history

- Browse collapsible day sections with a raid count for each day, newest first.
- Hover a day header to see completed raids versus other attempts.
- Expand a raid to view its room splits, timings, and party members.
- Filter by team size or search for a party member or raid status.
- Daily counts update to match your filters.
- Keep separate saved history for each RuneScape profile across client restarts.

Raids are grouped by their start date in your local time zone. The live raid appears separately from saved history.

## Compare raids

Click **Compare Raids** to open a separate window and select up to five saved raids. Compare room splits, combat time, time between rooms, total raid time, team sizes, and party members side by side.

Choose any selected raid as the baseline. Differences show how much faster (green) or slower (red) each time is than that run. Rooms compare by raid stage, with each cell identifying the boss fought:

| Stage | Bosses compared |
| --- | --- |
| Room 1 | Archive Guardian |
| Room 2 | Ezekiel or Bork & the Smorks |
| Room 3 | Moia or Dreadnoth the Jellied |
| Room 4 | Dagon'hai Elder and monks or Xarth |
| Room 5 | Ocellus |

For example, an Ezekiel split compares directly against a Bork split in Room 2. The Quartermaster visit does not add a boss row. Missing splits display a dash, and partial histories do not produce estimated combat or transition totals. Filter the selection list by team size, or search by date, party member, or status. Changing filters keeps your selected raids and baseline in the comparison so you can also compare different team sizes.

## Manage your records

- **Delete raid:** Right-click a saved raid card to remove the raid and its splits.
- **Delete room time:** Expand a saved raid and right-click a room row to remove that split. The recorded total raid time is preserved.
- **Undo last deletion:** Restore your most recently deleted saved raid or room time, including after restarting the client.
- **Reset current tracker:** Right-click the live raid card to discard the current attempt and wait for the next Archive Guardian start. Resetting the live tracker cannot be undone.

Deleted records stay removed when earlier game messages are replayed.
