## Installation

Open RuneLite's **Plugin Hub**, search for **TFA Splits Tracker**, and install the plugin.
You can also view its [Plugin Hub listing](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/tfa-splits-tracker).

## Features

- Automatically track server-reported room splits and total raid times.
- Track new Ocellus fights as Vines, Portals, Quadrants, and Prayer switches.
- View live timings in the sidebar, optional timing overlay, chat messages, and infoboxes.
- Customize the compact, transparent overlay's font, size, text color, and raid clock color
  under **Timing overlay**. Room times align on the right; optional raid splits appear beside
  room times, and Ocellus phases appear as indented rows. Unrecorded times show `--:--`.
- Browse saved raid history by day, team size, party member, result, or total raid time.
- Compare up to five raids side by side with time differences against a chosen baseline.
- Manage saved raids and splits, with undo for your last deletion.

History is saved separately for each RuneScape profile and kept between sessions.

Search by total raid time in both history and Compare Raids: `12:34` matches that second
(including hundredths), `<13:00` finds faster raids, and `12:00-13:00` finds an inclusive
range. Limits also support `<=`, `>` and `>=`, with optional hundredths (`12:34.56`).
Time searches combine with the team-size filter and keep selected comparisons in place.

Check **Hide Failed/Abandoned Raids** in history or Compare Raids to show only completed
raids. This also hides left and interrupted attempts, keeps saved records, and works
with search and team-size filters. Selected comparisons stay selected.
